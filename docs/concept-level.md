# The concept level

Four concept owners, one written brief. (The reference design uses four; adopt the number your project needs, keeping one brief.)

- Each human has a personal robot that captures their discussion and writes it into `concept_to_coordinator.md` - the one agreed brief between the concept owners and the delivery team.
- The Coordinator reads its orders from that brief, not from side conversations. One brief, one coordination queue.
- A personal robot can still talk directly to the Coordinator - questions, debugging, small adjustments to align expectations - but that direct line never overrides the agreed brief.
- If the Coordinator notices a request that really conflicts with the agreed concept, it does not just do it: it asks the concept owners to change the brief. Every concept change is recorded, so the project keeps a full concept change history.
- Clarifications cannot contradict the agreed concept. A major divergence requires a concept revision, recorded with its history.
- Humans need only two interaction lines into the delivery team: the Coordinator and QC. Everything else flows through the brief.
- Agreeing the concept first covers committed builds. Exploration, research and prototypes a human asks for during the concept phase are fine.
